# Todos

Todos are your inbox: the things Claude (or a scheduled job) needs **from you**. Instead of a question sitting in a session you aren't watching, it lands here — on your desktop and your phone.

Open it from the **Todos** icon in the sidebar, or the **Todos** tab on mobile. Todos waiting on you also appear on **Home** under **Needs you**.

## Kinds of todos

Every todo has an intent:

- **Decide** — Claude needs you to choose. Only you can close it.
- **Review** — something is ready for you to check.
- **Do** — something only you can do (log in somewhere, sign a form, buy a domain…).
- **FYI** — just letting you know.

Use the chips at the top to switch between **For me**, **AI working**, **Snoozed** and **Done**, and filter by project. **For me** groups todos into Decide, Review and Do.

## Where todos come from

- **You** — click **Add**, type what needs doing, pick an intent and (optionally) a project.
- **Claude** — during any session, when it needs something only you can give. It can attach files and Vault notes.
- **Jobs** — a scheduled run can create todos if the job has **Let it create todos for me** turned on, and a failed run creates a Review todo if **Create a todo when the run fails** is on. See [Jobs & Runs](./jobs.md).

## Responding

- **Approve** (Decide, Review) — optionally with a note, like "Go with option B".
- **Done** (Do, FYI).
- **Request changes** — tell Claude what to change. It continues the session that created the todo, or starts a new one.
- **Snooze** — hide it until this evening, tomorrow morning, or in 3 days.
- **Dismiss** — close it, with an optional reason.

Or just reply in the box at the bottom. If the todo came from a session, your reply goes straight back to that session. If it didn't, **Ask AI** starts a session in the todo's project to work on it.

When Claude finishes something you asked for on a todo, it posts the result on the thread and hands the todo back to you to check.

## What Claude can't do

Claude can reply to todos, report results and close todos that no longer need you. It can **never** approve, dismiss, reopen or delete a todo, and it can't close or snooze a **Decide** todo.

## Notifications

To get todos on your phone, turn on push notifications — see [Remote & Mobile](./mobile.md#push-notifications).
